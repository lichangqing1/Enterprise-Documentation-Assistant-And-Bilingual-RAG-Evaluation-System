# Enterprise Equipment Maintenance SOP / 企业设备维护标准操作规程

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-014  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-10-15  
**Document owner:** Facilities Operations Team  
**Classification:** Synthetic demonstration document — no real company or customer data

## 1. Purpose / 目的

This standard operating procedure defines a consistent process for inspecting, maintaining, reporting, and escalating issues with office and laboratory equipment. It is intended for internal training and for testing a documentation assistant.

本规程规定办公及实验室设备的检查、维护、故障报告与升级处理流程。本文件仅用于内部培训场景和文档问答助手测试。

## 2. Scope / 适用范围

The procedure applies to company-managed printers, meeting-room displays, desktop workstations, and nonhazardous laboratory measurement devices. It does not cover vehicles, medical devices, industrial high-voltage equipment, or emergency life-safety systems.

适用于公司管理的打印机、会议室显示屏、台式工作站和非危险性实验室测量设备。不适用于车辆、医疗设备、工业高压设备及紧急生命安全系统。

## 3. Maintenance schedule / 维护周期

- **Routine visual inspection:** Every **30 calendar days**.
- **Preventive maintenance:** Every **90 calendar days**.
- **Asset inventory verification:** Every **180 calendar days**.
- **Post-repair functional check:** Within **24 hours** after a repair is marked complete.
- **Inspection records:** Retained for **24 months** after the inspection date.

- **日常外观检查：**每 **30 个自然日**进行一次。
- **预防性维护：**每 **90 个自然日**进行一次。
- **资产盘点核对：**每 **180 个自然日**进行一次。
- **维修后功能检查：**维修标记完成后 **24 小时内**进行。
- **检查记录保留期：**检查日期起 **24 个月**。

## 4. Roles and responsibilities / 角色与职责

**Equipment users** report abnormal behavior, record the asset ID, and stop using equipment that appears unsafe. They must not open electrical enclosures or bypass safety protections.

**Facilities technicians** perform inspections, maintenance, diagnostic checks, and documented repairs. They record the inspection date, condition, action taken, and outcome.

**Facilities team lead** assigns priority, tracks overdue work, approves closure of critical incidents, and reviews recurring faults monthly.

**设备使用人员**报告异常情况，记录资产编号，并停止使用存在安全风险的设备；不得擅自拆卸电气外壳或绕过安全保护装置。

**设施维护技术人员**负责检查、维护、诊断和维修，并记录检查日期、设备状况、处置措施与结果。

**设施团队负责人**负责设定优先级、跟踪逾期任务、批准严重事件关闭，并每月复查重复性故障。

## 5. Incident severity and response targets / 故障等级与响应目标

| Severity | Definition | Initial response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Safety concern or a complete outage affecting a shared essential service | Within **2 hours** |
| High | Important service degraded with no practical workaround | Within **4 business hours** |
| Medium | Partial failure with a usable workaround | Within **1 business day** |
| Low | Cosmetic issue or nonurgent improvement request | Within **3 business days** |

**Critical incidents** must also be communicated to the Facilities team lead immediately. These are *initial response targets*, not guaranteed repair-completion times.

| 等级 | 定义 | 首次响应目标 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 严重（Critical） | 存在安全隐患，或关键共享服务完全中断 | **2 小时内** |
| 高（High） | 重要服务受损且无可行替代方案 | **4 个工作小时内** |
| 中（Medium） | 部分故障，但有可用替代方案 | **1 个工作日内** |
| 低（Low） | 外观问题或非紧急改进请求 | **3 个工作日内** |

严重故障还必须立即通知设施团队负责人。上述时间是**首次响应目标**，不代表维修完成时限。

## 6. Standard workflow / 标准工作流程

1. **Record:** Create an incident or maintenance ticket with asset ID, location, reporter, description, and observed symptoms.
2. **Classify:** Select severity using Section 5 and note any safety concern.
3. **Triage:** Check documented troubleshooting steps and determine whether the equipment must be taken out of service.
4. **Assign:** Route the ticket to a Facilities technician; escalate Critical issues immediately.
5. **Resolve:** Record actions, replacement parts (if any), completion time, and technician.
6. **Verify:** Perform a functional check within 24 hours of repair completion.
7. **Close:** The Facilities team lead approves Critical ticket closure; other tickets can be closed after documented verification.

标准流程为：**登记工单 → 分类定级 → 初步排查 → 分配处理 → 维修解决 → 功能验证 → 工单关闭**。严重故障必须立即升级，且需负责人批准关闭。

## 7. Required maintenance record fields / 维护记录必填字段

Each maintenance record must include: **asset ID, equipment category, location, inspection or incident date, reporter or technician, observed issue, severity (for incidents), action taken, verification outcome, and closure date if closed**.

每条维护记录必须包括：**资产编号、设备类别、位置、检查或故障日期、报告人或技术人员、问题描述、故障等级（如适用）、处理措施、验证结果，以及已关闭工单的关闭日期**。

## 8. Handling unsupported questions / 无依据问题处理

This document does **not** specify equipment purchase budgets, employee salaries, customer refunds, warranty prices, or supplier contract terms. A documentation assistant should not infer those values from this SOP; it should state that the information is unavailable in the supplied document.

本文件**不包含**设备采购预算、员工薪资、客户退款、保修价格或供应商合同条款。文档助手应明确说明提供的文件中没有这些信息，不应猜测。
